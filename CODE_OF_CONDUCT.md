# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

- Be respectful and patient.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you assume bad.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion on topic.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam or repeated off-topic posts.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests and any other space run for this project.

## Enforcement

- Maintainers may edit or remove comments and close threads that break these rules.
- Repeat or serious cases lead to a ban from the project spaces.

## Reporting

- Report a problem through a private channel: email security@saropa.com.
- Do not post it in a public issue.
- Reports are handled in confidence.
